The Root

It started with a man who found peace in a garden.

Artie Short — Vietnam Veteran — spent his life fishing, cooking, growing, and showing people how to do all of it. His daughter Stephanie watched. And never forgot.

For seventeen years, that observation became the engine behind everything built. A home garden became a community garden. A community garden became an apothecary business and the foundation of an intentional community. Those experiences produced foundational programs and a patent-pending capital readiness methodology that creates a roadmap for others seeking to develop a land-based enterprise and intergenerational wealth.

The Restoration Initiative is where all of it lands. South Carolina. Cooperative land ownership. A working farm and apothecary. A community that builds together, holds knowledge together, and sustains itself on ground that belongs to the people who tend it.

We have joined the Federation of Southern Cooperatives — 58 years of Black land retention, cooperative ownership, and advocacy. We are identifying land. We are ready to build.

We are inviting those who are ready to build alongside us.
